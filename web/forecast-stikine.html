Last season on the gauge — why we talk in odds

2025, the real sequence: opened Jul 31 on the glacier-melt recession, a rain system pushed it back over 550 on Aug 12, dry weather let it back down by Aug 25 for the rest of the season. That reclose happens in 4 out of 10 years here. The opening date is forecastable; whether it stays open is a coin the sky flips.

Why there's no April call here

We tested 70 years of snow data — it barely helps. This canyon runs on summer glacier-melt and rain, not winter snow. So the call sharpens as summer actually happens: ±10–12 days by mid-July, ±9–10 by Aug 1, day-precision only inside a week. Anyone selling you a Stikine date in April is selling you weather.
