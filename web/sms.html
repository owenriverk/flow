What the LateBoof text line is

LateBoof sends river gauge readings and flow alerts by SMS to whitewater paddlers in the United States and Canada. Two kinds of messages exist, and both require your opt-in first:

  • Gauge replies — you text us a river name, we text back one message with the current reading. Example reply: Middle Fork Salmon, At MF Lodge, ID · 2,800 cfs / 4.21 ft · USGS 13309220
  • Flow alerts — recurring notifications when a river you follow changes state (comes into its runnable range, spikes, or drops out).

Opt in to flow alerts

The checkbox is never pre-checked; submitting this form with the box checked is the sole opt-in mechanism for recurring alerts. We keep a timestamped record of your consent.

Opting in by texting us first

If you text a river name to the LateBoof toll-free number, that message is your opt-in to receive one reply per request with the reading you asked for. We send nothing else to your number unless you separately sign up for flow alerts above. Message and data rates may apply to each reply.

How to stop, get help, or reach a human

  • Reply STOP to any LateBoof message to unsubscribe immediately. You'll receive one final confirmation message and nothing further.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or email owen@lateboof.com any time.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Your phone number is used only to deliver the messages described above. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Full policy: lateboof.com/privacy.
